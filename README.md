## The entry

One sentence, 202 characters, pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a
tiny everyday annoyance — the midnight fridge raid:

> A fridge whose light dims to a single accusing spotlight whenever you open
> it after midnight, so your late-night snack is eaten under the silent
> judgment of an appliance that knows exactly what you did.

## Why this pitch

- **The invention is the joke.** The laugh comes from the fridge itself
  turning into a silent judge, not from a punchline bolted on at the end.
- **The fix is silly yet weirdly plausible.** A dimming light that shames
  you out of a midnight snack is absurd, but the mechanism — light as
  social pressure — is exactly how guilt already works at 2 a.m.
- **Original.** Not an alarm clock, not a complaining sock, not a sighing
  kettle: an appliance that knows what you did and says nothing.
- **Tight.** One sentence names the annoyance (the midnight fridge raid),
  the invention (the judgmental light) and its silly mechanism (the
  accusing spotlight) without clutter.

## How it runs

`main.py` is a standard-library-only program that copies the pre-written
`entry.txt` into the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default
`out`). It imports nothing beyond `os` and `shutil`, uses no network, and
writes nothing into its own directory.
